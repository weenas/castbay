- **Live streams no longer pause by themselves.** Casting a live video (sports and other live streams) from an iPhone could stop every minute or two, because the TV told the iPhone a position that jumped back and forth. It now reports a live stream's position as growing steadily, with no duration.
- **Mirroring on TVs whose video decoder refuses a speed-up setting.** On some TVs (a Rockchip one on Android 11 was reported) the screen stayed black while the sound played, because the decoder would not start with CastBay's low-delay setting and CastBay gave up. It now starts the decoder again without that setting.
- **The home screen stays while a phone connects.** Instead of a black screen with one line, the status says "Connecting to" and the phone's name until the music or mirroring screen appears.
- **A one-time question about statistics and error reports.** The first time you open the app, a card on the home screen asks whether to send anonymous statistics and error reports. Both are off unless you turn them on there, and Settings, under General, changes them at any time. The privacy page says what is sent.
- Smaller: the home screen's "Desktop casting" is now "PC casting" so it fits its column.
