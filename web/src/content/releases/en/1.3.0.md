- About → Open-Source Licenses lists what CastBay is built from (UxPlay, libplist, Apple's ALAC, OpenSSL, Media3 and more), each with its license's full text.
- Encryption now uses OpenSSL 3.5 (long-term support until 2030), built from source; the OpenSSL 1.1.1 used before is no longer maintained, and its license didn't fit the GPL.
- If uploading a problem report fails, Diagnostics says why (for example "timed out" or "could not connect") and records it, and the upload is tried once more first.
- Video and music playback (Media3) and the screens (Jetpack Compose) are updated to their latest versions.
