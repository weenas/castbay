- **Mirroring keeps the TV awake.** The TV no longer goes to its screen saver while a screen is mirrored (it did on some Sony TVs, which dim and switch to the saver after a while without a key press).
- **H.265 from CastBay Desktop.** The receiver tests its H.265 decoder when it starts and tells the computer only what it can really decode. Computers that can send H.265 (set in CastBay Desktop's settings) then use it; H.264 stays the default.
- The first line on the home screen now says the PC is a source of casts too ("AirPlay, DLNA and PC receiver"), and the Chinese settings call the program 映湾桌面端.
