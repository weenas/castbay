- Touch screens (car displays, tablets): tap the playing screen to open or close the quick menu; its remote-key hint is hidden, and nothing looks pre-selected while you use touch. Help describes taps and buttons instead of the remote.
- Media buttons, a car's steering-wheel buttons among them, now pause and change tracks: CastBay holds Android's audio focus while casting, so the system sends them to it, and the car's own player pauses. If another app starts playing, CastBay's music or video pauses.
- The quick menu closes by itself after five seconds untouched.
- Videos cast from apps get Pause (showing where the video is) and Back/Ahead 10 seconds in the quick menu.
- CastBay now describes itself as an AirPlay and DLNA receiver for Android TVs, car displays and tablets.
