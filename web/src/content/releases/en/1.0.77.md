- **Music is played when the phone means it to be heard**, as on an Apple TV: lyrics stay in sync, resuming after a pause no longer repeats or skips a stretch, and the first seconds no longer stutter.
- **Casting verification**: not required, confirm on the TV, a PIN the first time (as on an Apple TV), or a password; each device that has cast can be allowed or blocked.
- Mirroring stats add the phone's own report: frames sent and dropped, round trip, packet loss and bandwidth.
- When another device takes over, the screen shows its name.
- Settings are on tabs (Connection, Screen Mirroring, Playback, General), with a language choice and Restore Defaults; mirroring options say what Auto picks (up to 2160p).
- A red dot on About when a newer version is out, with a QR code to download it.
- Back twice on the home screen quits; the "Receive casts" switch is gone.
- AirPlay music loses its 10-second skip buttons (the protocol can't seek exactly; use the phone).
- A larger purple banner on the TV's home screen, listed with video apps.
- The last line of lyrics is no longer cut off.
