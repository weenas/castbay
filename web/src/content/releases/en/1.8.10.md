- **Casting wakes the TV from its screen saver.** With **Open When Casting** on (Settings → Playback), a cast that starts while the TV shows its screen saver now wakes the TV and opens CastBay, so the picture is there without pressing a key on the remote. Before, the cast started behind the screen saver and the screen stayed black. It needs the same "Display over other apps" permission as Open When Casting already did.
- For troubleshooting, the log now says how far apart the sender's key frames are.
