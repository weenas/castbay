- On devices that sleep instead of shutting down, such as car displays, CastBay tries to start receiving again when the device wakes: when its clock is synced, or within about 15 minutes once it has a network, without being opened by hand.
- Diagnostics also shows each of those start attempts and how CastBay's previous run ended (closed by the system, stopped, low memory…), to see why it isn't running.
