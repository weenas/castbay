- Videos cast from apps (AirPlay or DLNA, a series from iQiyi for example) have a seek bar in the quick menu: drag it to jump anywhere, or, with a remote, press Up to reach it and Left or Right to move it.
- DLNA music's progress bar can be dragged too. AirPlay music's can't: the phone plays it out, and AirPlay gives the TV no way to choose a time (the FAQ explains).
- Taps on a playing video or mirrored screen open the menu more reliably on touch screens.
- Short screens, such as car displays and phones on their side: the home screen's Settings, Help and About buttons, About's details and the music controls (with lyrics too) all stay in view.
- On-screen help mentions the seek bars.
