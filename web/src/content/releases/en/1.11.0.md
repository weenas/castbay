- **Updated the app name and branding.** CastBay now uses localized names based on the system language, with refreshed TV banners and brand assets.
- **Wake the TV for AirPlay music from standby.** Starting music while the TV is in standby wakes the screen. Music played over a screen saver keeps the existing behavior and does not force the receiver UI open.
- **Prevent accidental receiver use on phones.** CastBay is intended for TVs and large-screen devices. On phones it shows an explanation and does not start the receiver in the background or upload device reports.
