- CastBay now runs on Android 6.0 and later (it needed 8.0), so older TVs can use it too: tested on a Xiaomi TV 4 on Android 6.0.1, with mirroring from an iPhone and music.
- With a remote, Left and Right move a video's seek bar (in the menu under Down) and DLNA music's seek bar by 10 seconds a press, 30 while held; the thumb turns white when it has the focus.
- Added to the compatibility list: Xiaomi TV 4.
