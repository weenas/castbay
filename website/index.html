Android TV 投屏接收器A casting receiver for Android TV

把 iPhone 的画面和声音，投到你的 Android 电视上 Cast your iPhone to your Android TV

映湾（CastBay）让 Android 电视接收 AirPlay 屏幕镜像、音乐和视频，也支持视频和音乐 App 自带的投屏按钮（DLNA）。免费、开源、无广告。 CastBay lets an Android TV receive AirPlay screen mirroring, music and video, and the cast button in video and music apps (DLNA). Free, open source, no ads.

为电视和遥控器设计，从手机投过来的画面、音乐和视频都有合适的呈现方式。Designed for the big screen and the remote: mirroring, music and video each get a screen made for them.

三步开始使用Get started in three steps

电视和手机需要连接同一个网络。Your TV and phone need to be on the same network.

在电视上安装Install on your TV

从 GitHub 下载映湾的 APK，用 U 盘或电视上的文件管理器安装。Download the CastBay APK from GitHub and install it from a USB drive or the TV's file manager.

打开映湾Open CastBay

打开就开始接收投屏，以后电视一开机也会自动就绪，不用再手动打开。It starts receiving as soon as it opens, and from then on it's ready whenever the TV turns on.

从手机投屏Cast from your phone

在 iPhone 控制中心选"屏幕镜像"，或在 App 里点 AirPlay 图标或投屏按钮，选择 CastBay（默认设备名，可在设置里修改）。On an iPhone, choose Screen Mirroring in Control Center, or tap the AirPlay icon or cast button in an app, then pick CastBay.

两种投屏方式Two ways to cast

同一个 App 往往两种都支持，按需选择。Many apps support both; pick whichever suits you.

常见问题FAQ

支持哪些电视？Which TVs are supported?

Android 8.0 及以上的 Android TV 和 Google TV 设备，包括电视和电视盒子。已在 TCL（Android 9）和索尼（Android 12）电视上测试。Android TV and Google TV devices, TVs and boxes alike, on Android 8.0 or later. Tested on TCL (Android 9) and Sony (Android 12) TVs.

如何把 APK 安装到电视上？How do I install the APK on a TV?

把 APK 拷到 U 盘插到电视上，或用电视上的文件管理器（例如 ES 文件浏览器）打开安装。第一次安装时，电视可能会提示允许"安装未知来源的应用"，按提示打开即可。Copy the APK to a USB drive, or open it with a file manager on the TV (ES File Explorer, for example). The first time, the TV may ask you to allow installing apps from unknown sources.

手机上找不到电视怎么办？My phone doesn't find CastBay. What now?

确认电视和手机在同一个 Wi-Fi 下，并且映湾已经打开并显示"等待投屏连接"。有些路由器开启了"AP 隔离"，会阻止设备互相发现，需要在路由器设置里关闭。也可以试试把手机的 Wi-Fi 关掉再打开。Make sure the TV and phone are on the same Wi-Fi and CastBay is open, waiting for a connection. Some routers enable "AP isolation", which stops devices finding each other; turn it off in the router's settings. Turning the phone's Wi-Fi off and on can also help.

支持 Google Cast（Chromecast）或 Miracast 吗？Does it support Google Cast (Chromecast) or Miracast?

不支持。Google Cast 只能由 Google 认证的设备接收，Miracast 需要电视系统级的权限，普通 App 无法实现。映湾支持的是 AirPlay 和 DLNA。No. Only Google-certified devices can receive Google Cast, and Miracast needs system-level access an app doesn't get. CastBay supports AirPlay and DLNA.

为什么哔哩哔哩投屏只有 720P？Why is Bilibili limited to 720p?

哔哩哔哩只把 1080P 及以上的清晰度开放给它的官方电视 App，投到第三方设备最高是 720P。想看 1080P 可以用屏幕镜像。Bilibili keeps 1080p and above for its own TV app; casting to other devices tops out at 720p. Use screen mirroring for 1080p.

怎么用遥控器结束投屏？How do I stop a cast with the remote?

连按两次返回键，或按下键打开快捷菜单，选择"结束投屏"。按主页键则会让投屏在后台继续播放。Press Back twice, or press Down for the quick menu and choose Stop casting. Pressing Home keeps the cast playing in the background.

映湾会收集我的数据吗？Does CastBay collect my data?

不会。映湾不需要账号，也没有统计或广告。只有在你打开"显示歌词"时，才会把歌名和歌手发送到 lrclib.net 查询歌词。详见隐私政策。No. There's no account, analytics or ads. Only when you turn on lyrics does it send the song's title and artist to lrclib.net to look them up. See the privacy policy.
