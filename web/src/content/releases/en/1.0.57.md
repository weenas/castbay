- Video cast from apps (YouTube, for example) starts sooner and reaches high quality sooner; up to 4K on 4K TVs.
- YouTube casts again after an earlier video had finished.
- AirPlay music is buffered, so Wi-Fi hiccups don't make it stutter.
- Lyrics: long lines scroll, "No lyrics" where there are none, and the screen no longer jumps while they load.
- The TV remote controls the phone's music reliably, including on iPhones whose buttons did nothing before; pausing from the TV is instant, and the cover stays while paused.
- The TV leaves the playing screen when the phone goes away.
- A volume bar on the TV when the phone's volume changes.
- A compact music screen on small TVs (540 dp tall).
- NetEase Cloud Music's song titles show correctly over DLNA.
