- Fixes CastBay closing when an iPhone starts casting on Android 6 (seen on a Xiaomi TV).
- Problem reports now include the last log lines of a previous run that ended unexpectedly (a crash, or Android closing CastBay), on every Android version, so one report shows what went wrong. CastBay keeps a few hundred KB of recent log on the device for this; see the [privacy policy](/privacy).
- Problem reports from Android 6 carry the app's system log lines.
