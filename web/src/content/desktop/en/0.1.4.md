- **Diagnostics** (Settings → About): this computer's facts and the end of the log, and **Upload log**, which asks first and gives a report ID to put in your problem report. Network addresses, receivers' names and your user folder are taken out first.
- **Send error reports** (off by default): after the program ended badly it asks whether to send a report (or sends it, if you turn the switch on), and so for a cast that failed because of the encoder or the screen capture.
- **Send anonymous usage statistics** (off by default): one summary a day, with no names and no addresses; the privacy policy lists every field. Turning it off deletes what was sent.
- **Switch what is cast** while casting (Windows): choose another window or screen from the casting page, without ending the cast. If the window being cast is closed, the picture stays and the cast waits for a new choice.
- **Quality** is now a bitrate slider (2 to 20 Mbit/s) and a frame rate of 30 or 60 fps, instead of three fixed choices; earlier choices are kept as the matching values.
- **The list of receivers** shows each one's address and, for receivers on CastBay 1.8.9 or later, its model and version; receivers this computer has paired with are marked; and a line says that it is looking while it does.
- **The encoder list** says what each is (graphics card or CPU) and lists only what works on this computer. The drop-down lists are readable again on Ubuntu's dark theme.
- **Updating on Linux** asks for your password in the system's own box, installs the new version and starts it again; a **What's new** link next to Update opens this page.
- Smaller: minimise the window when a cast starts (a switch, on by default), a switch for checking for updates at start, Restore default settings, a Report a problem link.
