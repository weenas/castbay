- About → Diagnostics shows what this device is and what recently happened to CastBay: when it started (and whether the device had just booted), boot broadcasts, keys and steering-wheel buttons, taps and audio focus. It helps find problems on devices that can't be debugged from a computer, such as car displays: photograph it and send it with a report. It stays on the device and can be cleared.
