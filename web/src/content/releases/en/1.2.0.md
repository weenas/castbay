- About → Diagnostics → Upload log sends a problem report (this device's details, recent events and the app's log, with titles, device names, links and internet addresses taken out first) after showing what will be sent. You get a report ID and a QR code that opens the feedback page with it filled in.
- If CastBay stopped unexpectedly, the home screen asks whether to send a report about it. To send such reports without being asked, turn on Send Error Reports (Settings → General, off by default).
- Anonymous usage statistics, off unless you turn them on (Settings → General): daily counts of casts and failures, the device's model and Android version, never names, titles or links. Turning it off deletes what was sent. Every field is in the privacy policy.
- When a newer version is out, About's What's New lists every change since your version, before you update.
- On the music screen, a long phone name scrolls instead of covering the song's title, and the line no longer says "connected".
- About and Settings fit narrow screens, such as a tablet held upright.
