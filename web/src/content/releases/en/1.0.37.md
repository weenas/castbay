The first public release.

- **AirPlay screen mirroring** at up to 60 fps, and H.265 4K on 4K TVs.
- **AirPlay music** with a blurred-cover backdrop, optional synced lyrics and round controls; **AirPlay video** played by the TV itself.
- **DLNA**: the cast button in Bilibili, iQiyi, NetEase Cloud Music and more, from iPhones and Android phones.
- A quick menu while playing: picture fit, playback stats, audio and subtitles; Back twice ends a cast.
- A casting password, and refusing or allowing a second device to take over.
- English and Chinese, a Help screen, and ready whenever the TV turns on.
