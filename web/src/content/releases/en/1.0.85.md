- The quick menu (Down or Menu while casting) is now a compact bar along the bottom of the screen, in the app's colours, so it covers far less of the picture. Left and Right choose an option, OK changes it, Back or Down closes it. Stop casting is gone from it: press Back twice.
- Leaving a Mac's screen mirroring from the TV goes straight back to the home screen (it could stop on a black screen that took two more presses of Back).
- Screen mirroring asks the TV's decoder to show each frame as soon as it is decoded, and the playback stats show how long decoding takes.
- New FAQ entry: why mirroring lags a little, and how to cut it (the TV's Game picture mode; 1080p for a Mac). In our tests a MacBook mirrored to a Sony 4K TV in under 100 ms.
